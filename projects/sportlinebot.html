02The problem

The problem

Marking the lines of a sports field by hand is slow and imprecise. SportLineBot drives and sprays paint on its own while an operator supervises from a browser, with buttons or by typing a sentence in French such as "trace un terrain de foot".

03What I built

What I built

  • Firmware for an ESP32-S3 with a WebSocket server, time-based motion, an ultrasonic sensor on a servo for obstacle scanning, a paint pump and a paint-level sensor.
  • Flask and Socket.IO dashboard with a live field view that follows the 7 steps of a football field (outer rectangle, halfway line, centre circle and both penalty and goal areas).
  • An NLP intent classifier (TF-IDF character n-grams and Multinomial Naive Bayes, 8 intents, about 130 French phrases) that turns a sentence into a robot command.
  • Anomaly monitoring with safety rules first (paint empty, obstacle closer than 15 cm, low paint) and an Isolation Forest second.
  • A mock ESP32 so the dashboard can be tested without the robot.

04Results

Results

  • Hackathon prototype at reduced scale; only the football field is implemented and the motion is open loop, so it must be calibrated.
  • The team received a certificate of participation at the MECA WORLD CUP, organised by the ENSA Tétouan Mechatronics Club.
