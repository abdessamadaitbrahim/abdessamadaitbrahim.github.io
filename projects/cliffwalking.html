Value Iteration vs Policy Iteration

Dynamic programming for reinforcement learning on CliffWalking-v1: convergence, speed and optimal-policy comparison.

The problem

Compare two dynamic-programming algorithms for optimal decision-making on the 4 x 12 CliffWalking-v1 grid from Gymnasium: how fast each converges and whether they reach the same policy.

What I built

  • Value Iteration with Bellman optimality updates (threshold 1e-6, at most 5,000 iterations).
  • Policy Evaluation and Policy Iteration, with optimal policies extracted from the environment's transition model.
  • Policy evaluation by simulation and animated trajectories of the optimal agent.

Results

  • Both algorithms converge to the same optimal policy, with a mean reward of -13.00 in about 15 iterations.
  • Value Iteration took about 0.0094 s against about 0.3259 s for Policy Iteration, roughly 35 times faster in the recorded run (times depend on hardware).
