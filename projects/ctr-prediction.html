CTR Prediction: Machine Learning from Scratch

Decision Tree, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes and SVM implemented from scratch with NumPy and compared with scikit-learn.

The problem

Predict whether a user clicks an online advertisement from demographic, behavioural and contextual features. The point of the project is to understand the mathematics of four classical algorithms by implementing them without a machine-learning library.

What I built

  • Exploratory analysis of 10,000 rows, with 215 exact duplicates removed (9,785 left) and high-cardinality categorical features prepared for modelling.
  • From-scratch NumPy implementations of a Decision Tree (Gini impurity, recursive best-split search), Logistic Regression (batch gradient descent), Naive Bayes and SVM.
  • Comparison between the from-scratch models and their scikit-learn counterparts with accuracy, confusion matrices and classification reports.

Results

  • The three validated from-scratch models (Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, SVM) reach about 0.86 test accuracy.
  • The Decision Tree reaches about 1.00 train accuracy against about 0.82-0.83 test accuracy, a clear case of overfitting.
  • A preprocessing bug in one scikit-learn evaluation is documented in the repository rather than hidden.
