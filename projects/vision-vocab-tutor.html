02The problem

The problem

Vocabulary is easier to remember when it is tied to what the learner can see. The application turns real-world photos into quizzes whose questions are about what is actually visible, and it brings back the words the learner gets wrong at the right time.

03What I built

What I built

  • An object detector (YOLOv8s, run locally) reads each picture; a vision LLM then writes a multiple-choice question about what is visible. The LLM chain uses Groq first and OpenRouter as a fallback.
  • Four themed environments (classroom, kitchen, supermarket, fruits) and four levels, with a session of 10 questions and difficulty that adapts to the learner's results.
  • A personal vocabulary bank with SM-2 style spaced-repetition scheduling and flashcard review sessions.
  • A dashboard with score evolution, level distribution, per-environment statistics and a next-level prediction.
  • Accounts with hashed passwords and a login lockout after 5 failed attempts.
