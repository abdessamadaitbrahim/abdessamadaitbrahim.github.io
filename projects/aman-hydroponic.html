02The problem

The problem

Irrigation wastes water: the team's pitch cites the FAO figure that 70% of the water used in irrigation is lost, and about 2,000 litres of water to grow 1 kg of vegetables. AMAN aims to optimise water management and make agriculture more accessible with a vertical system that recirculates its water and monitors itself.

03What I built

What I built

  • An AgriTech solution built in 48 hours by Team AMAN: a smart vertical hydroponic tower with closed-loop irrigation.
  • A real-time IoT dashboard for pH, EC (electrical conductivity), temperature and solar energy.
  • AmanBot, an AI diagnostic chatbot for crop management.
  • Predictive AI for decision support, with solar power for energy autonomy.
  • A pitch with a business model canvas, cost structure, financial analysis and a 6-month roadmap: prototype optimisation, pilot phase, impact measurement and preparation for deployment.

04Results

Results

  • Semi-finalist (top 38 of 125+ teams) at the Water4Future International Hackathon 2026, and winner of the jury's 2026 W4F Students Hackathon Water Talent Prize.
  • Hackathon prototype: there is no public code repository.
