Internship project

Botground: Multilingual Sentiment Analysis

Sentiment analysis of chatbot conversations in French, English and Moroccan Darija, with fine-tuned transformers and a Flask dashboard.

The problem

Customer conversations handled by a chatbot arrive in French, English and Moroccan Darija written in Latin letters. Darija has no standard spelling, uses numbers as letters (3, 7), mixes with French in the same message and has few labelled resources. The goal was to score messages and whole conversations and to track customer satisfaction.

What I built

  • Fine-tuned and compared three multilingual transformers on 4,199 labelled messages: XLM-RoBERTa base, mBERT and DarijaBERT, all with the same training setup (3 epochs, batch size 16, early stopping on macro F1).
  • Three sentiment classes (negative, neutral, positive) across customer support, hospitality and logistics domains.
  • Scored conversations message by message, with a majority vote for the conversation label and the share of positive messages as the satisfaction rate.
  • Built a Flask dashboard that scores messages and conversations in real time and tracks satisfaction over time.

Results

  • XLM-RoBERTa base is the best model: 0.843 accuracy and 0.842 macro F1 on 840 test messages (mBERT 0.834, DarijaBERT 0.817 macro F1).
  • The Darija specialist does not win on Darija: DarijaBERT reaches 0.832 macro F1 on Darija messages against 0.889 for XLM-RoBERTa.
  • The training data is confidential and is not published; the repository shows aggregate statistics only.
