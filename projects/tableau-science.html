02The problem

The problem

Tell the story of the world's scientific production between 1996 and 2014 from SCImago country indicators: documents, citations, H-index and rank.

03What I built

What I built

  • A Tableau Story with three dashboards: contribution of the leading countries, analysis by country (with a world production map) and an H-index overview.
  • Interactive controls: indicator selector (documents or citations), year and size parameters, country filters and highlight actions.
  • Published on Tableau Public.
